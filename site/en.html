AI / UNDER THE HOOD

Models, keys and generation limits.

AI currently connects through ZenMux. Base URL: https://zenmux.ai/api/v1. Save your ZenMux API Key in Settings → ZenMux AI. Separate OpenAI, Google or MiniMax keys are not required. ZenMux bills model calls against your account; local editing requires no subscription.

These are preset IDs in the current code, not a guarantee of availability. Check the ZenMux catalog, account access and balance. Model lists are editable. Storyboard shots span 1–30 seconds and split at configured model limits. Standard video requests validate 1–120 seconds, but providers may impose lower limits. There is no universal resolution guarantee for other models. AI output resolution is separate from project and export resolution.

Each shot can optionally pass its last frame as the next shot’s first-frame reference. The route must support image references; visual or character continuity is not guaranteed. Prompts and reference media are sent to the provider. Keys stay locally and are excluded from project exports.
