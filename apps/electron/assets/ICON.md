A single native macOS rounded-square tile centered with standard generous transparent outside margin (tile occupies approximately 82% of canvas), smooth continuous rounded corners, midnight navy enamel surface, restrained subtle dimensional highlight. On tile: a bold sculptural silver-white editing ribbon forming an open C around a small luminous cyan right-pointing play triangle; two neat overlapping ribbon segments subtly suggest video timeline clips. Original, elegant, professional, instantly readable at 32px. Large simple geometry, strong silhouette, modest depth, straight-on view. No lettering, no text, no brand from another company, no screenshot, no scenery, no surrounding frame or desktop. Actual transparent background outside tile. Keep all main artwork comfortably within the tile; no glow spilling outside it.
